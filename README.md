# Bálint Mazzag

Hungarian national health insurance data: cohort definitions,
survival analysis, propensity scores and Bayesian models, mostly in R.

Recent work

  *European Journal of Heart Failure*, 2026
- Long-term outcomes of teriparatide treatment on hip fracture risk,
  *Journal of Bone and Mineral Research*, 2026
- Treatment gaps in paediatric ADHD pharmacotherapy, 10-year nationwide
  cohort (preprint): https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-10411233/v1

Tools: R (tidyverse, Quarto, Shiny, package development), SQL, Python, Git, Docker
